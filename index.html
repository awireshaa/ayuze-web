HOW WE WORK

Brief to delivery, in four steps.

The same process runs underneath every service — whether it's a wedding, a livestream or a screen install.

Brief

You tell us the event, date, venue and what "done well" looks like. We recommend the right mix of services.

Plan

We confirm crew size, equipment (cameras, screens, stream gear) and a shot list or run of show.

Produce

On the day, we shoot, stream and manage the screens — quietly, on time, without getting in the way of your event.

Deliver

You get edited footage, raw files where agreed, and stream recordings — on the timeline we agreed upfront.

What we need from you, and when.

The earlier we know the venue and internet situation, the fewer surprises on the day.

Scope the stream

We confirm platforms, expected length, number of cameras and whether an LED relay screen is needed.

Venue & connectivity check

We check the venue's internet and power, and plan a backup connection if needed.

Test stream

FORMATS

Three ways to run a conversation.

Studio Conversation

Host and guest seated together in a controlled setup — consistent lighting, backdrop and audio from episode to episode. Best for recurring interview series or podcasts.

On-Location Interview

We bring lighting, audio and cameras to your guest — an office, a home, or an event floor. Best for founder profiles, vox pops and one-off features.

Remote / Hybrid Guest

One speaker in-studio, one joining remotely, composited into a clean two-shot for the final edit. Best when a guest can't travel to Lagos.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Every episode, produced the same way.

  • — Two or more cameras: a wide two-shot plus individual angles on each speaker
  • — Lapel or boom microphones, recorded separately for a clean mix
  • — Three-point lighting set for studio sessions
  • — Full-episode edit with colour grading and audio mix
  • — Short vertical cutdowns for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts
  • — Captions/subtitles burned in for social clips
  • — Cover art and thumbnail frame for each episode
  • — Raw interview footage available on request

ABOUT AYUZE STUDIO

A media crew built around one idea: fewer vendors, better coordination.

AYUZE Studio is an events media and technology company based in Oshodi, Lagos. We cover event videography, LED screens, video production, livestreaming and interviews — because most events need several of these at once, and coordinating separate vendors for each one is where things go wrong.

HOW WE STARTED

Started around a camera, grew around the whole event.

AYUZE Studio began with event videography — covering weddings and corporate functions — and grew into LED screens, livestreaming and interview production as clients kept asking for more of their event's media to be handled by one team instead of three.

HOW WE WORK

Three things we don't compromise on.

One point of contact

Whether it's cameras, screens or the stream, you brief one team and one team is accountable for the result.

Backup, always

A second camera angle, a second internet connection, a second battery — the plan assumes something can fail.

Deliver on the date agreed

Edits and stream recordings go out on the timeline we set at the brief, not "whenever it's ready."
